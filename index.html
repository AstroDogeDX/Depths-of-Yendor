How to play

Moving

WASD move · Mouse look · ← → turn

Shift sprint: fast, but loud.

C sneak on or off: slow and near-silent, for striking sleepers unaware, and for creeping over traps you've found without setting them off. Sprinting stands you back up.

Fighting

Click or Space attack. Your swing meter refills between blows, and a partial swing hits softer.

F grip your weapon in both hands, or one. In both, it needs less strength, so a heavy weapon is easier to wield and a light one hits harder, but your lantern hangs at your belt.

Right-click hold to raise a shield: it takes some of each blow from in front, at the cost of your stamina, and a click shoves with it · or, with a bow, hold to nock an arrow, then hold click to draw and let go to shoot · Q drink known healing · R / T artefact powers

Everything else

E pick up, open, buy, use stairs · I pack · M map

1–4 hotbar (put things there from your pack): scrolls and food are used at once · potions and wands: hold the key, then click to throw or zap, right-click to drink or zap yourself · stones, darts and knives: hold the key, hold right-click to draw back, and click to throw · a weapon, lantern, shield or bow there swaps into your hand · P pixel size · Esc pause, or save and quit

The descent

    Potions, scrolls, wands and rings are unidentified: their colours, labels and woods are shuffled every run. Drink, read and wear at your own risk. Some equipment is cursed and will not come off. Monsters sleep until they notice you. Strike first, and strike hard.

    Your run saves on every floor you reach, when you save and quit, and when you close the page: Continue picks it up from the title screen. Dying ends it for good.

    Play fullscreen if you can. In Chrome and Edge, Esc then pauses without leaving fullscreen: hold it down to leave.
